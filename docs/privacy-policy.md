# AI Radar Backup — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

## Overview

AI Radar is a personal AI research project. Its Google Drive integration is used to store and verify backups of the application's database.

## Information accessed

With your authorization, AI Radar uses Google's Drive API with the `drive.file` permission. This allows the application to create and access files associated with the application.

## Information stored

AI Radar backups may contain research discoveries, source information, analysis results, pricing history, notification delivery records, workflow history and backup metadata.

These backups are stored in the user's Google Drive.

## Data sharing

AI Radar does not intentionally sell or share backup contents with third parties. Access to the application and its credentials should remain under the user's control.

## Data deletion and revoking access

The user can revoke the application's Google account access through their Google Account security settings and delete backup files from Google Drive.
